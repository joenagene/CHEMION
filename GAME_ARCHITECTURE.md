### 1. Main Menu
- Player opens CHEMION: The Last Cure.
- Main Menu appears.
- Options: **Start Game, Instructions, Continue Game (if saved progress exists), and Exit**.
- Selecting Start Game opens Character Selection.

### 2. Character Selection
- Player chooses one of the five available scientists.
- Each scientist has a unique appearance and character description.
- Player confirms the selected scientist.
- The game begins at Level 1.

### 3. Level 1 — The Outbreak (Abandoned Town)
- Player enters a large, explorable abandoned town.
- Player moves using WASD while the camera follows.
- Player explores streets, buildings, and contaminated areas.
- Zombies roam the battlefield and detect nearby players.
- Player searches for chemicals, supplies, and research notes.
- Collected materials are stored in the inventory.

**Zombie Encounter:**
- When a zombie detects the player, it begins chasing.
- Player can run away or engage in combat.
- If a zombie reaches the player, it deals damage.
- Repeated attacks reduce the player's HP.
- When HP reaches 0, the player dies and the Game Over screen appears.

**Potion Mixing:**
- Player accesses a laboratory station.
- Player selects available chemicals and a discovered potion recipe.
- The game presents a chemistry-based challenge.
- Correct answers and sufficient ingredients produce a potion.
- Incorrect answers cause the mixing attempt to fail.
- Successfully crafted potions are stored in the inventory.
- Potions can be used against zombies or for recovery, depending on their type.

**Level Completion:**
- Complete the required chemistry challenges.
- Collect essential research materials.
- Defeat the level's main zombie enemy.
- Reach the designated exit.
- Level 2 becomes available.

### 4. Level 2 — The Contaminated Hospital
- Player enters an abandoned hospital filled with infected patients.
- Player explores medical rooms, laboratories, and restricted areas.
- Stronger zombies appear with different attack patterns.
- Player collects chemical compounds and medical research records.
- Chemistry challenges focus on compounds and chemical formulas.
- Player creates improved potions using collected ingredients.
- Complete the hospital objectives and defeat the main enemy.
- Unlock Level 3.

### 5. Level 3 — The Toxic Factory
- Player explores a damaged chemical factory.
- Toxic spills and hazardous areas create additional obstacles.
- Mutated zombies appear and deal increased damage.
- Player collects chemicals and materials from storage areas.
- Chemistry challenges involve mixtures, pH, and concentration.
- Player develops potions for stronger enemies.
- Complete the factory objectives and defeat the main enemy.
- Unlock Level 4.

### 6. Level 4 — The Lost Research Facility
- Player enters an abandoned research facility.
- Player discovers information about the chemical outbreak.
- Advanced zombies guard important laboratory areas.
- Player collects research documents and cure components.
- Chemistry challenges involve chemical bonding and reactions.
- Player prepares advanced potions and gathers the remaining cure materials.
- Complete the research objectives and defeat the main enemy.
- Unlock Level 5.

### 7. Level 5 — The Final Cure (Genesis Laboratory)
- Player enters the final laboratory where the outbreak originated.
- The environment contains dangerous chemical hazards and powerful zombies.
- Player collects the remaining ingredients for the final cure.
- Chemistry challenges involve stoichiometry and concentration.
- Player completes the final potion-mixing challenge.
- Player encounters the final boss zombie.
- The final boss requires the correct potions and combat strategy to defeat.

**Final Mission:**
- Defeat the final boss.
- Complete the final cure formula.
- Activate the cure distribution system.
- The outbreak is contained.
- The victory ending appears.

### 8. Zombie Combat System
- Zombies move around the battlefield.
- Zombies detect and chase the player within a certain range.
- Contact with zombies causes damage, with a short cooldown between hits.
- The player may escape or fight using available potions.
- Different zombie types have different HP, damage, and weaknesses.
- Correct potions deal damage or apply special effects.
- Defeated zombies may drop materials or supplies.
- The player continues exploring after surviving an encounter.

### 9. Health and Death System
- Player begins with a set amount of HP.
- Zombie attacks and environmental hazards reduce HP.
- Recovery potions restore HP.
- When HP reaches 0, the player dies.
- The Game Over screen appears.

**Game Over Options:**
- **Retry** — Restart from the most recent checkpoint.
- **Main Menu** — Return to the main menu.
- **Exit** — Close the game.

### 10. Inventory System
- Stores collected chemicals, potions, research notes, and supplies.
- Tracks ingredient quantities.
- Prevents crafting when required ingredients are missing.
- Removes ingredients consumed during successful potion mixing.
- Allows players to select and use available potions.

### 11. Checkpoint and Progression System
- Progress is saved at designated checkpoints.
- Completed levels remain unlocked.
- Player retains saved progression when continuing the game.
- Dying returns the player to the most recent checkpoint.
- Completing a level unlocks the next location.

### 12. Victory and Ending
- Player successfully creates the final cure.
- Final boss is defeated.
- Cure distribution is activated.
- The chemical outbreak is contained.
- The ending story and **GAME COMPLETED** screen appear.
- Player may return to the main menu or exit the game.

---

### Overall Game Flow

**Launch Game → Main Menu → Start Game → Character Selection → Level 1 → Explore Battlefield → Collect Materials → Encounter Zombies → Escape or Fight → Mix Potions → Complete Chemistry Challenges → Defeat Level Boss → Unlock Next Level → Repeat for Levels 2–5 → Create Final Cure → Defeat Final Boss → Victory Ending**

**Failure Flow:** Zombie Attack / Environmental Damage → HP Reaches 0 → Game Over → Retry Checkpoint or Return to Main Menu.
